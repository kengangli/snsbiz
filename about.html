About Us

A Sydney-based tax and consulting practice built around clarity, accountability and commercial outcomes.

Who we are

SNS Biz is a boutique professional services firm that combines tax expertise with business consulting. We work with sole traders, family businesses, growing SMEs and established companies who want a trusted partner — not just a tax preparer.

We believe small and medium businesses deserve advice of the same calibre offered to large corporates: rigorous, lateral, and grounded in commercial reality. That belief shapes how we hire, how we work, and how we charge.

How we work

Every engagement starts with understanding the business. From there, we design a service mix that's right-sized to your stage and ambition — never bloated, never under-resourced.

You'll deal directly with a senior consultant who knows your file, returns your call, and is accountable for outcomes. No call centres, no juniors learning on your business.

Our values

·

Clarity

Plain English advice, real options, and clear next steps. We translate tax and finance into decisions you can act on.

·

Accountability
